# 7. Conclusion

Much of what misled our agents and verifiers was never the model. It was a warped formatter nobody had checked.

We compared warped wording with clear wording while holding each tool result fixed. Agents made far fewer unsupported claims from the clear wording in the scope, completeness, and uncertainty families, while provenance changed little. Verifiers reversed their verdicts on the same false answers under the clear wording, and the control showed why: given the missing facts and asked about correctness, they caught everything. They were blind, not weak.

Who verifies the tools? Today, mostly nobody. We need to compare what the tool did with what its output formatter actually tells the model.

The appendix includes the short prompt we used with ordinary coding agents to find warped formatting in source code. It was cheap to run, and it worked on systems that were not ours. If you have an agentic system, consider trying it.
